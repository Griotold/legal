Pengu Pop Support

Questions, feedback, or a problem with Pengu Pop? We can help.

Email gotjd9773@naver.com. For technical issues, include your device model, iOS version, app version, and what happened. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

How do I play?

Tap penguins as they appear, avoid bombs, and build combos during a 30-second round. Golden and diamond penguins award more points. A missed penguin or a bomb resets your combo.

What does Remove Ads include?

This optional one-time purchase removes banner and interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads remain available. Open Settings > Remove Ads to see the current App Store price.

How do I restore Remove Ads?

Use the Apple Account associated with your purchase, then open Settings > Remove Ads > Restore Purchases. Check your internet connection if restoration fails. Apple handles payments and refund requests.

Can I keep playing when a rewarded ad is unavailable?

You can continue to the result screen and start a new round. The optional 10-second extension is granted only after earning the ad reward and closing the ad, at most once per round.

Why is the leaderboard unavailable?

Game Center features need an authenticated Game Center account and network access. Check Game Center in iOS Settings. You can still play without Game Center. Previously saved leaderboard data may be shown when fresh data cannot be loaded; it may not reflect current rankings.

Where are my records saved?

Your personal best and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app may remove those records. Game Center scores and App Store purchases are managed separately by Apple.

Sound and haptics

Open Settings to adjust sound-effects volume or enable and disable haptics. Music is currently marked Coming soon.
