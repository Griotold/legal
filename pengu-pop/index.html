Pengu Pop

Tap penguins. Build combos. Beat your best.

A quick arcade game on a snowy 3-by-3 board. Find golden and diamond penguins, avoid bombs, and keep your combo going before the 30-second timer runs out.

Free to download and play on iPhone. The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase removes banner and interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available.

Compare your best score through Apple Game Center. No app account is needed to play. Game Center features require a Game Center account and network access.

The game interface is in English.
