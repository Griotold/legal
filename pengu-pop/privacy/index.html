Pengu Pop Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Pengu Pop is a short arcade game that you can play without creating an account. This policy describes the current version of the app as of the effective date below.

1. Game data on your device

The app stores your best score, number of games played, sound-effects volume and on/off preference, haptics preference, and animation preference locally on your device. Your current score, combo, and remaining time are used to run a game session. We do not operate a game-account server or upload your game progress to our own server.

The app uses local storage and bundled sound and image files. It does not require access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location. The current version does not include an advertising, analytics, or in-app purchase SDK and does not request tracking permission.

2. Features marked Coming soon

Music and Remove Ads may appear in Settings as features that are not yet available. Selecting these entries does not start a purchase or activate advertising. The current test continuation feature does not show a real rewarded advertisement.

If advertising, purchases, or other services that change data processing are introduced, we will update this policy to describe the services, information processed, and available choices before those features are enabled.

3. External websites and platform services

Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links open a website in your browser. These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process information such as your IP address and browser request information when you visit them, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies to these policy pages.

Apple may process information when you use its devices, App Store, backups, or diagnostics, according to your settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy. Such platform services are separate from the game’s local storage.

4. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Game records and preferences remain in the app’s local storage until its data is removed. Deleting the app normally removes its local data; operating-system backups may retain or restore it according to your settings. We cannot retrieve or delete your local game records remotely. You can change sound, haptics, and animation preferences in Settings.

We do not sell or share your locally stored game records for targeted advertising. If you email us, we use the email address and message you provide to respond to your request. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for support.

5. Children and privacy requests

The current game has no account or in-app personal-information form. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a child has sent by email. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us. These requests can be sent to the contact address below.

6. Changes and contact

Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. For Pengu Pop privacy questions or requests, email gotjd9773@naver.com.
