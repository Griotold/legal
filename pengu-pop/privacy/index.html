Pengu Pop Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Pengu Pop is a short arcade game that you can play without creating an account. This policy describes the current version of the app as of the effective date below.

1. Game data on your device

The app stores your best score, number of games played, sound-effects volume and on/off preference, and haptics preference locally on your device. An animation preference from an earlier version may remain in local data for compatibility; game animations now run automatically. Your current score, combo, and remaining time are used to run a game session. We do not operate a game-account server or upload your game progress to our own server.

The app uses local storage and bundled sound and image files. It does not require access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location. The app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising and consent choices. It uses Apple StoreKit to offer an optional Remove Ads purchase. The current version does not request Apple tracking permission.

2. Advertising and privacy choices

Pengu Pop displays banner and occasional interstitial advertisements. You may choose to watch a rewarded advertisement to extend a round. Ads require an internet connection and may not always be available.

Google’s advertising SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device or app identifiers, diagnostic and performance information, advertisements shown, and interactions with ads and the app. Google uses this information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and service improvement. We do not send your locally stored scores to an account server operated by us.

UMP requests consent information and presents privacy choices where required. When available for your region, you can revisit these choices through Settings → Privacy choices. Your choices, device settings, and Google’s requirements affect which ads can be requested. Not granting tracking permission does not mean that all advertising data processing stops.

For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising. Google manages its own retention and privacy controls. Remove Ads disables banner and interstitial advertisements; optional rewarded advertisements remain available.

3. Purchases, external websites, and platform services

Apple processes in-app payments. Pengu Pop checks StoreKit-verified purchase entitlements to activate or restore Remove Ads; we do not operate a purchase-verification server or receive your payment-card details. Diagnostic logs record purchase stages, outcomes, and error codes, without Apple Account details, receipts, tokens, or raw transaction payloads. Purchase history is managed by Apple separately from locally stored game records.

Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links open a website in your browser. These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process information such as your IP address and browser request information when you visit them, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies to these policy pages.

Apple may process information when you use its devices, App Store, backups, or diagnostics, according to your settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy. Such platform services are separate from the game’s local storage.

Game Center leaderboards

If you authenticate with Apple Game Center, the app submits final round scores to Apple and reads leaderboard ranks, scores, player identifiers, and Game Center display names. Your display name and score may be visible to other players through the leaderboard. The app does not request or display your Apple Account email address. It caches leaderboard data on your device to show previously loaded results when a fresh request is unavailable. Cached rankings may be out of date. Apple manages Game Center accounts and online records under its privacy policy and platform controls. Local personal-best records remain separate from Game Center records.

4. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Game records and preferences remain in the app’s local storage until its data is removed. Deleting the app normally removes its local data; operating-system backups may retain or restore it according to your settings. We cannot retrieve or delete your local game records remotely. You can change sound and haptics preferences in Settings.

We do not sell or share your locally stored game records for targeted advertising. If you email us, we use the email address and message you provide to respond to your request. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for support.

5. Children and privacy requests

The current game has no account or in-app personal-information form. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a child has sent by email. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us. These requests can be sent to the contact address below.

6. Changes and contact

Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. For Pengu Pop privacy questions or requests, email gotjd9773@naver.com.
