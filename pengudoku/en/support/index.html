Support

Updated: October 4, 2026 · Preview

Contact us

Send questions or bug reports to gotjd9773@naver.com. Include the app version shown in Settings, device model, operating-system version and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information first.

How do I play?

Place one penguin in every row, column and colored region. Penguins cannot touch, even diagonally. Open How to play in the app for the controls and rules.

How is the language chosen?

By default, Pengudoku uses the system language supplied by your device: English, Korean, Japanese or German. If that language is not supported, English is used. Settings → Language lets you choose a language manually; your choice is saved. Choose the system setting again to restore automatic selection.

Where is my progress?

Progress and preferences stay on this device. There is no account or cloud-sync service. Removing app data may erase your progress, and we cannot retrieve it from a server.

Why is an ad unavailable?

The preview uses test advertisements. Availability depends on the network and SDK. If a rewarded ad is unavailable or closed before the reward is earned, no reward is granted; you can continue playing without watching it.

Can I remove ads or restore a purchase?

Not yet. Both options are Coming Soon in this preview. No payment is taken and there are no Pengudoku purchases to restore.
