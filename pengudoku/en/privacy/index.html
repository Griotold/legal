Privacy Policy

Updated: October 4, 2026

About this policy

Pengudoku is a logic puzzle game provided by Griotold. No account is required. This policy covers advertising and the optional Remove Ads purchase in the iOS release. Development builds use test ads; purchases are unavailable in the Android preview.

Game data on your device

Progress, saved puzzles and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a backend to collect or sync this game data. Removing the app or its data may erase progress. Operating-system backups may retain or restore it according to your settings.

Advertising and choices

Pengudoku uses Google Mobile Ads and requests non-personalized ads. The SDK may process IP addresses, identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. A consent form is shown where required. When available, you can revisit Privacy options in Settings. Removing interstitial ads does not disable optional rewarded ads or all SDK processing. Google Privacy · iOS SDK · Android SDK.

Purchases

Apple processes iOS payments. The app uses StoreKit to verify purchase and refund status and restore the Remove Ads entitlement. We do not receive your payment card details or operate a purchase-account server.

Support and your choices

If you contact us, your email address and message are used to answer your request. Correspondence is retained while needed for support or applicable obligations, then deleted when no longer needed. Contact gotjd9773@naver.com for access, correction or deletion requests and other privacy questions. Please do not send passwords or payment details. Google-held data is subject to Google’s policies and request processes.

This website and changes

These pages are prepared for GitHub Pages. When hosted there, GitHub may process technical connection information; see GitHub Privacy. We add no advertising or analytics scripts. Changes will appear here with a new update date.
