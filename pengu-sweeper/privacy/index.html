Pengu Sweeper Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

Pengu Sweeper is a puzzle game that does not require an account. Game progress, coins, and preferences are stored on your device. We use Google AdMob for advertising, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising consent, and Apple's App Store services for in-app purchases.

1. Information We Process

Your current level, completed levels, coin balance, and sound, music, and haptic preferences are stored in the app's local storage to support gameplay and remember your choices. The developer does not collect this game data on a separate server or synchronize it with a game account.

Google AdMob may process approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app identifiers, advertising and app interactions, and diagnostic and performance information to deliver and measure ads, diagnose issues, and prevent fraud. The information processed depends on the SDK, your region, your device, and your consent choices. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures for iOS.

2. Advertising and Your Choices

The game may show interstitial ads between levels. Rewarded ads for coins or continuing a game are optional and play only when you choose to watch them. You can play the regular game without watching rewarded ads.

Remove Ads disables forced interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads remain available, and the purchase does not disable all processing by third-party services.

The app uses UMP to determine whether advertising consent is required. Where applicable, you can review or change your choices through Ad Privacy Choices in the home settings. The app currently does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission.

3. Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. The app uses StoreKit to retrieve product information and verify purchase transactions and your Remove Ads entitlement, including when you restore purchases. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase and account information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Storage and Deletion

Game progress, coins, and preferences remain on your device until the app or its data is deleted. Your operating system may back up and restore this data depending on your settings. Deleting the app does not delete purchase records held by Apple. Information processed by Google and Apple is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.

5. Contact and Privacy Requests

The developer does not sell your game progress or coin balance or separately send that information to advertising services. If you contact us by email, we process your email address and message to respond to your request and delete information that is no longer needed once the request is resolved.

For privacy questions or requests to exercise your privacy rights, contact gotjd9773@naver.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its third-party services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
