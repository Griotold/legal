Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Onda: Binaural Beats ("Onda") is provided by griotold ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy covers the Onda iOS app, these policy pages, and information you choose to send us.

1. Your listening stays on your device

Onda does not require an account or ask for your name or email address to listen. Your saved mixes, mix names, selected sounds, frequencies, volume levels, listening preferences, and recent listening history are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or synchronizes this information.

The app generates and plays audio on your device using included sound files. It does not record your microphone or access your contacts, location, photos, or Apple Health data. Playback information is passed to iOS to provide system controls such as the Lock Screen player.

Onda does not provide its own cloud backup. Apple's device backup and transfer features may include app data, depending on your settings. We cannot retrieve those backups for you.

2. App Store purchases

If purchases are available in your version of Onda and you make or restore an App Store purchase, Apple processes the payment and provides purchase information needed to confirm access. This can include a product identifier, transaction identifier, purchase date, subscription expiration, and refund or revocation status. That information is used to validate or restore the purchased access, not to build a listening profile.

We do not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password. Restoring a purchase does not send us your saved mixes or restore them from an Onda account. Apple's handling of purchases is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Support messages

When you contact us, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you send. We use these to answer you, investigate reported problems, and handle related requests. Please do not send passwords, full payment details, or personal information unrelated to your request.

Email is delivered and stored through our email provider, Naver, and the services used by you. These providers handle messages under their own terms. We do not use support messages for unsolicited marketing.

4. Analytics, advertising, and this website

Onda has no advertising SDKs, third-party behavioral analytics SDKs, or cross-app advertising tracking. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Apple may make aggregated or non-personally-identifying app statistics and diagnostic reports available to developers according to its policies and your settings. Any such reports we consult are used to understand app reliability and fix problems. You can control diagnostic sharing in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. See Apple's App Analytics information.

These policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them sends a web request to GitHub; GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security and may process other technical request information under its Privacy Statement. We add no analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, or contact forms to these pages. See GitHub's explanation of Pages data collection.

Opening an App Store page, an email app, or another external link is governed by that service's privacy practices. These services may process information in countries other than your own. Information stored only in Onda on your device is not sent to them merely because you use the audio player.

5. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local settings and mixes remain on your device until removed or replaced. Recent listening entries are limited to the most recent 20 and older entries are replaced. Deleting a saved mix does not necessarily remove a matching entry from recent history or the current mix. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading may keep it. Device backups remain subject to your Apple settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription or erase Apple's purchase records.

We retain support correspondence while it is needed to address your request, resolve related issues, or meet applicable legal obligations, then delete it. We may disclose information we hold if required by law, limiting disclosure to what is necessary.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, or restrict or object to the use of personal information we hold. Email us to exercise those rights or raise a concern. We may need to verify that a request comes from the person concerned. You may also contact your local data protection authority. We cannot remotely access or erase data held only on your device, or change records held independently by Apple or GitHub.

We use reasonable safeguards for support information, but no device or email service can be guaranteed completely secure. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it and remove information where appropriate.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. We will provide additional notice or request permission where required before making a material change to how personal information is used.

For questions or privacy requests, visit our Contact us page to reach griotold.
