Sudoku — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy explains how Sudoku handles information. Sudoku does not require an account. We do not operate a server that collects your puzzles or gameplay history.

1. Information stored on your device

Sudoku stores your current puzzle, entered numbers, notes, mistakes, elapsed time, hint usage, undo history, completion statistics, unlocked difficulties, and preferences on your device. This information supports continuing a game and remembering your settings. We do not upload it to our own servers.

2. Advertising services

Sudoku includes Google AdMob for advertising. Advertising availability depends on the app version and region. The current development version uses test advertising; production advertising is not yet enabled.

When advertising is requested, Google's SDK may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic and performance information to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. This does not mean that your puzzle history is sent to us.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures for details. Sudoku does not currently request Apple's tracking permission. We will update this policy and provide applicable consent controls before enabling production advertising.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases and purchase restoration are not available in the current version. Sudoku does not collect payment card details. If purchases become available, this policy will be updated to explain the purchase service and information involved.

4. Retention and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed, for example when you delete the app and its data. Device backups may retain or restore app data according to your operating system settings. Sudoku does not provide account-based cloud synchronization. Third-party advertising data is retained under the relevant provider's policies.

You can change sound, haptics, and gameplay preferences in Settings. You can contact us with privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information you have sent us. Rights may vary by your location; we cannot retrieve local gameplay data that has not been sent to us.

5. Support and external pages

If you email us, we use your email address and message to answer your request and retain the correspondence as needed for support. Please do not send sensitive information. Policy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages; visiting them is subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. For privacy questions, contact gotjd9773@naver.com.
