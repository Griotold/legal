Sudoku — Support

Need help with Sudoku: Train Your Mind? Email gotjd9773@naver.com.

Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Continue a puzzle

Return to the home screen and select Continue. The game stores your puzzle and progress on your device. Deleting the app may remove this data; the app does not provide account-based cloud synchronization.

Unlock the next difficulty

Complete three puzzles at the previous difficulty. For example, three Easy completions unlock Medium.

Use hints

Each puzzle includes three free hints. Hint shows the reasoning before you choose whether to apply the answer. Revealing an explanation uses a hint even if you close it without applying. Additional hints may use an optional rewarded ad.

Restore Remove Ads

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase, then open Settings and select Restore Purchases. You can also restore from the Remove Ads dialog. The purchase removes banner and automatic full-screen ads; optional rewarded hint ads remain available.

Sound and haptics

Adjust sound effects and haptics separately in Settings.
