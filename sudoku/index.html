Sudoku: Train Your Mind

Take a quiet moment with classic number puzzles, a calm nature-inspired setting, and step-by-step logical hints.

Start with Easy and unlock Medium, Hard, Expert, and Master as you complete puzzles. Use notes, undo, and erase, continue a saved puzzle, and follow your progress in Statistics.

Free to play on iPhone in portrait orientation. The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase removes banners and automatic full-screen ads. Optional rewarded hint ads remain available. No account is required. The game interface is in English.
