Ice Rescue Support

Questions or problems? Email gotjd9773@naver.com with your device model, iOS version, app version, and what happened. Do not include passwords or payment details.

How to play

Move the paddle horizontally and tap to launch the ball. Break the ice and rescue every animal in the level.

Music and sound

New installations start with music and sound effects at 0%. Adjust either slider during onboarding or in Settings. Haptics can be toggled separately.

Remove Ads and restore

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase that removes forced interstitial ads. Optional rewarded Continue ads remain available. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase and select Settings > Restore Purchases to restore it.

Daily Challenge

Complete all 80 levels to unlock Daily Challenge. Challenges refresh at your device's local midnight.

Saved progress

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app may remove your progress. The Apple-managed Remove Ads purchase can be restored separately.
